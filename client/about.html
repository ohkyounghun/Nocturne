About Nocturne

Nocturne is a community platform for discovering and sharing the best night view spots in Korea. Beautiful night views are scattered across blogs, Instagram, and word of mouth — Nocturne brings them all to one place.

Browse spots on an interactive map, submit your own hidden gems, like and bookmark your favorites, and connect with others who love the city after dark.

How to Use

  1. 01
    Browse the Map

    Explore Korea's night view spots on the interactive map. Click any pin to see details.

  2. 02
    Create an Account

    Register to unlock full features — submit spots, like, bookmark, and comment.

  3. 03
    Submit a Spot

    Found a hidden gem? Submit it with a location, photo, and description to share with the community.

  4. 04
    Like & Bookmark

    Like spots to signal quality. Bookmark your favorites to save them to your personal collection.

  5. 05
    Leave a Comment

    Share tips, recent conditions, or personal experiences on each spot's detail page.
